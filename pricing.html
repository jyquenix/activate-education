Pricing

Each tier is a monthly package of 4 one-hour sessions.

Included in every tier

  • Free initial assessment.
  • Individualized lesson plans built from scratch for each student, based on the assessment. We do not use a fixed curriculum or packaged program.
  • Unlimited texting with Liz (cofounder).
  • Monthly planning meeting: in the first week of each month, a cofounder (Liz or Vicky) meets each family on Google Meet. The meeting covers the goals met, the new goals, and the plan for the next month.
  • Tutor check-in: before each monthly meeting, the cofounder meets the student's tutor (when the cofounder is not the tutor) to review progress.
  • The plan is updated every month based on input from both the parents and the tutor.

Tier 1: Certified Teacher

$600 per month (4 one-hour sessions, $150 per hour)
Additional hours: $100 per hour

  • One-to-one tutoring with a certified teacher.
  • All items included in every tier.

Tier 2: Certified Teacher with School Collaboration

$800 per month (4 one-hour sessions, $200 per hour)
Additional hours: $150 per hour

  • One-to-one tutoring with a certified teacher.
  • All items included in every tier.
  • Individualized homework.
  • The student's school curriculum, worked into the plan as needed or on request.
  • School collaboration:
    • We contact the student's teachers at the start of the school year, then by email and phone during the year as needed.
    • We attend IEP meetings and parent-teacher conferences.

Tier 3: Cofounder

$1,000 per month (4 one-hour sessions, $250 per hour)
Additional hours: $200 per hour

  • One-to-one tutoring with a cofounder (Liz or Vicky).
  • All items included in every tier.
  • Individualized homework.
  • The student's school curriculum, worked into the plan as needed or on request.
  • School collaboration, as in Tier 2.

Additional Hours

Families can add hours to any package at $50 less than their tier's hourly rate. Common reasons:

  • A month with 5 of the regular session day (for example, 5 Mondays), so the 5th session is added.
  • Extra preparation before a test.
